**Grounded context:** Her remit includes merchandising capabilities, product development, assortment design, and partner collaborations.

I noticed Target named you Chief Merchandising Officer, with responsibility spanning merchandising capabilities, product development and assortment design.

Would you be open to a 15-minute call to see if it's a fit?

StyleSense AI
